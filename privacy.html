This is a personal portfolio. It has no sign-up, no contact form, no cookies of its own and no analytics or ad trackers. I only get your information if you choose to email or message me, and I use it only to reply to you.

Information you send me

If you email me, message me on WhatsApp, or contact me through LinkedIn, Fiverr or Instagram, I receive what you choose to send, such as your name, email address, phone number and project details.

  • I use it only to reply to you, discuss your project, and send quotes or work you asked for.

Information collected automatically

I don't run analytics, tracking pixels or advertising on this site. Like every website, though, a few services are involved in showing it to you:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may log your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Fiverr's image servers deliver some of the pictures on the home page, so they also receive a standard request from your browser. See Fiverr's privacy policy.

I don't receive or see any of this technical data.

Links to other sites

This site links to Fiverr, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp and fluentlish.academy. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave this site.

You can ask me at any time what information I hold about you from our messages, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email yousufj1010@gmail.com and I'll reply within 7 days.
